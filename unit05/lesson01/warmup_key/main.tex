\begin{notesbox}{Getting Ready: Ideas We'll Use Today}
\small These three ideas set up today's $3\times3$ determinant. Answer quickly.

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{1. A $2\times2$ determinant (Lesson 5.0).} Recall
$\det\begin{bsmallmatrix} a & b\\ c & d \end{bsmallmatrix}=ad-bc$. Compute
\[
  \det\begin{bmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix} = {\color{keyred}\mathbf{(3)(2)-(1)(1)=5}}.
\]

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{2. Volume of a box.} A rectangular box (prism) is $2$ long, $3$ wide, and $4$ tall. Its
volume is
\[
  \text{volume} = \text{length}\times\text{width}\times\text{height} = {\color{keyred}\mathbf{2\cdot3\cdot4=24}}.
\]

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{3. Crossing out a row and column.} In
$A=\begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 3 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$, cross out \emph{row 1} and
\emph{column 1}. Write the $2\times2$ matrix that is left, then compute its determinant.
\ansline{What is left is $\begin{bsmallmatrix} 3 & 1\\ 1 & 2 \end{bsmallmatrix}$, and $\det=(3)(2)-(1)(1)=5$.}
\end{notesbox}

\begin{teachernote}
All three items are the pieces of cofactor expansion. Item~1 is a $2\times2$ determinant (the minor
they will compute three of). Item~2 primes ``volume $=$ product of the dimensions,'' the geometric
meaning of a $3\times3$ determinant. Item~3 rehearses the exact motion --- cross out a row and
column, read off the $2\times2$ minor --- and its answer ($5$) is the \emph{first} term of the notes'
worked example $\det A=8$. Watch for $ad+bc$ in item~1.
\end{teachernote}
