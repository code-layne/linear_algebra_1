\begin{remindbox}
\small \textbf{Elimination} turns a system into an easy one. The \textbf{pivot} is the coefficient
you build on; the \textbf{multiplier} $\ell = \text{(entry to clear)} \div \text{(pivot)}$ tells you
how many pivot rows to subtract to make a zero. Clear every entry below each pivot to reach an
\textbf{upper-triangular} system, then \textbf{back-substitute} from the bottom up. A $0$ in a pivot
spot? \textbf{Swap} in a row from below.
\end{remindbox}
