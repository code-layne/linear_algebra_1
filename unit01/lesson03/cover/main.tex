\begin{remindbox}
\small A \textbf{matrix} is vectors standing side by side as \textbf{columns}. Multiplying by a
vector combines those columns: $A\mathbf{x}=x_1\mathbf{a}_1+x_2\mathbf{a}_2$. The
\textbf{column space} $C(A)$ collects \emph{every} such combination --- the span of the columns.
Two independent columns fill the whole \textbf{plane}; two parallel columns fill only a
\textbf{line}.
\end{remindbox}
