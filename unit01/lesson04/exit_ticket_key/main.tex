Show your work.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=14pt]
  \item Multiply \emph{by columns}:
        $\begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 3 \\ 2 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$.
        Compute each column of the product as a combination of the first matrix's columns.
        \ansline{Col~1: $1\begin{bsmallmatrix} 2\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}+2\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 4\\3 \end{bsmallmatrix}$. \ Col~2: $3\begin{bsmallmatrix} 2\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}+1\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 7\\4 \end{bsmallmatrix}$. \ Product $=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 4 & 7\\3 & 4 \end{bsmallmatrix}$.}
  \item Factor $A=\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 1 & 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix}$ as $A=CR$. Which columns are
        \textbf{independent} (they go in $C$)? Build the recipe $R$, and give the \textbf{rank}.
        \ansline{Columns~1 and~2 ($\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix},\begin{bsmallmatrix} 2\\3 \end{bsmallmatrix}$) are independent; column~3 $=$ col~1 $+$ col~2 ($\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}+\begin{bsmallmatrix} 2\\3 \end{bsmallmatrix}=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 3\\4 \end{bsmallmatrix}$). So $C=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1 & 2\\1 & 3 \end{bsmallmatrix}$, $R=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1\\0 & 1 & 1 \end{bsmallmatrix}$, rank $2$.}
\end{enumerate}

\begin{teachernote}
Item~1 checks ``multiply by columns'' --- full credit needs each product column shown as a
combination of $\begin{bsmallmatrix} 2\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}$ and $\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}$,
not a row-by-row mash. Item~2 is the whole factorization: independent columns $\to C$, weights that
rebuild each column $\to R$, and rank $=$ columns in $C$. A common slip is putting the dependent
column~3 into $C$; $C$ holds only the independent columns.
\end{teachernote}
