\begin{remindbox}
\small To \textbf{find} the singular values and vectors of \emph{any} matrix $A$, build the symmetric
matrix $A^{\top}A$. Because it is symmetric, it has real, nonnegative eigenvalues $\lambda_1\ge\lambda_2\ge0$
and perpendicular eigenvectors (Unit~6). The \textbf{singular values} are $\sigma_i=\sqrt{\lambda_i}$, the
\textbf{input singular vectors} $\mathbf{v}_i$ are those eigenvectors, and the \textbf{output singular
vectors} are $\mathbf{u}_i=A\mathbf{v}_i/\sigma_i$ --- automatically perpendicular unit vectors. Collect
them into $A=U\Sigma V^{\top}$. The recipe needs nothing but $A^{\top}A$, so it works for a matrix of
\emph{any} shape.
\end{remindbox}
