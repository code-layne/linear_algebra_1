\begin{notesbox}{Practice}
Show each determinant. Recall $\det\begin{bsmallmatrix} a & b\\ c & d \end{bsmallmatrix}=ad-bc$;
the \textbf{area} of the parallelogram spanned by the columns is $|\det|$.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.9em, itemsep=12pt]
  \item \textbf{Compute determinants.}
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=4pt]
      \item $\det\begin{bmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 2 & 4 \end{bmatrix}$ \hfill \ans{$12-2=10$}
      \item $\det\begin{bmatrix} 5 & 2 \\ 0 & 3 \end{bmatrix}$ \hfill \ans{$15-0=15$}
      \item $\det\begin{bmatrix} 2 & 6 \\ 1 & 3 \end{bmatrix}$ \hfill \ans{$6-6=0$}
    \end{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Area from columns.} Find the area of the parallelogram spanned by the two columns.
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=4pt]
      \item $\begin{bmatrix} 4 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix}$ \hfill \ans{$|12-0|=12$}
      \item $\begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix}$ \hfill \ans{$|12-1|=11$}
    \end{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Orientation \& the swap rule.}
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=6pt]
      \item Compute $\det\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 4 \\ 2 & 3 \end{bmatrix}$. Kept or flipped? \quad \ans{$3-8=-5$; flipped (negative).}
      \item Swap the columns to get $\begin{bmatrix} 4 & 1 \\ 3 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$ and compute its determinant. Compare. \quad \ans{$8-3=5$; the negative of (a) --- swapping columns flips the sign.}
    \end{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Zero determinant \& invertibility (§2).} Find $x$ so that
        $A=\begin{bmatrix} 2 & 6 \\ 3 & x \end{bmatrix}$ has $\det=0$. Is $A$ invertible for that $x$?
        What do the two columns look like?
        \ansline{$2x-18=0\Rightarrow x=9$. Not invertible: the columns $\begin{bsmallmatrix} 2\\3 \end{bsmallmatrix}$ and $\begin{bsmallmatrix} 6\\9 \end{bsmallmatrix}=3\begin{bsmallmatrix} 2\\3 \end{bsmallmatrix}$ are parallel, so the box is flat.}
  \item \textbf{Explain.} What does the \emph{sign} of $\det A$ tell you about a $2\times2$ matrix?
        What does $\det A=0$ mean about its columns and its inverse?
        \ansline{The sign is orientation: positive keeps the plane's turning direction, negative \emph{flips} it (a reflection). $\det A=0$ means the columns are parallel (zero area), so $A$ has \emph{no inverse}.}
\end{enumerate}
\end{notesbox}

\begin{extensionbox}
\textbf{Area-scaling factor (a first look at transformations).} Applying a matrix $A$ to the
\textbf{unit square} (area $1$) produces the parallelogram spanned by $A$'s columns, whose area is
$|\det A|$. So $|\det A|$ is the factor every area is multiplied by (Lesson~5.3).
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=5pt]
  \item For $A=\begin{bmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$, find the area of the image of the unit square. By what factor did area grow?
  \item For $B=\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$, find $\det B$. What is the area factor, and what does the sign say happened to the square?
\end{enumerate}
\ansline{(a) $\det A=(3)(2)-(1)(1)=5$; image area $=5$, so area grew by a factor of $5$.}
\ansline{(b) $\det B=(1)(1)-(2)(2)=-3$; area factor $|-3|=3$, and the negative sign means the square was \emph{flipped} (reflected).}
\end{extensionbox}

\begin{teachernote}
Item~1(c), 2, and 4 all reuse the ``parallel columns $\Rightarrow\det=0$'' idea from different
angles. Item~4 is the §2 callback (no inverse when $\det=0$); item~5 is the target justification
(sign $=$ orientation; zero $=$ parallel/no inverse). The extension is the Lesson~5.3 preview:
$|\det|$ is the area-scaling factor and a negative determinant is a reflection. Most common slips:
$ad+bc$, dropped absolute value on ``area,'' and calling a negative determinant ``no area.''
\end{teachernote}
