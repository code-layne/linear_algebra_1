\textbf{1. $A\xx$ is a combination of the columns.} With $A=\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$
and $\xx=\begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$, compute $A\xx = 2\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1\\3 \end{bsmallmatrix}+1\begin{bsmallmatrix} 2\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}$:
\[
  A\xx = \begin{bmatrix}\rule{0pt}{1.1em}\blank{1.1cm}\\[2pt]\blank{1.1cm}\end{bmatrix}.
\]

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{2. The distributive law $A(\xx+\yy)=A\xx+A\yy$.} Keep the same $A$, and take
$\yy=\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}$. Fill both sides and check they match.
\[
  A(\xx+\yy)=A\begin{bsmallmatrix} 3\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\rule{0pt}{1.1em}\blank{1.0cm}\\[2pt]\blank{1.0cm}\end{bmatrix},
  \qquad
  A\xx+A\yy=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 4\\7 \end{bsmallmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}\rule{0pt}{1.1em}\blank{1.0cm}\\[2pt]\blank{1.0cm}\end{bmatrix}
  =\begin{bmatrix}\rule{0pt}{1.1em}\blank{1.0cm}\\[2pt]\blank{1.0cm}\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Are the two sides equal? \; \textbf{yes} \quad / \quad \textbf{no}
