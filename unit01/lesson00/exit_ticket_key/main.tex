Let $\vv = \begin{bmatrix} 5 \\ 12 \end{bmatrix}$ and $\mathbf{u} = \begin{bmatrix} -4 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$.
Show your work.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=14pt]
  \item Find $\vv + \mathbf{u}$ and $3\mathbf{u}$.
        \ansline{$\vv+\mathbf{u}=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1 \\ 13 \end{bsmallmatrix}$;\quad
                 $3\mathbf{u}=\begin{bsmallmatrix} -12 \\ 3 \end{bsmallmatrix}$}
  \item Find $\|\vv\|$, the length of $\vv$. Show the Pythagorean step.
        \ansline{$\|\vv\|=\sqrt{5^2+12^2}=\sqrt{169}=13$}
  \item Maya says the vector $\begin{bmatrix} -7 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}$ has length $-7$.
        Explain her mistake and give the correct length.
        \ansline{A length is a distance, so it is never negative. The formula squares each
        component: $\sqrt{(-7)^2+0^2}=\sqrt{49}=7$. The correct length is $7$.}
\end{enumerate}

\begin{teachernote}
Item 3 checks the most common misconception --- treating a negative component as a negative
length. Full credit needs both ``length can't be negative'' \emph{and} the correct value $7$.
\end{teachernote}
