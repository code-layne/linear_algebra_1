\begin{notesbox}{Getting Ready: Skills We'll Use Today}
\small Today we \emph{save} elimination as a product $A=LU$ (lower times upper). These three moves are
the pieces. Answer quickly.

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{1. Lower times upper} (Lesson 1.4). Multiply, one column at a time:
\[
  \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 4 & 1 \end{bmatrix}
  \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 5 \\ 0 & 3 \end{bmatrix}
  = \begin{bmatrix}{\color{keyred}\mathbf{2}} & {\color{keyred}\mathbf{5}}\\[2pt]{\color{keyred}\mathbf{8}} & {\color{keyred}\mathbf{23}}\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Notice a lower-triangular times an upper-triangular matrix rebuilds a \emph{full} matrix.
\hspace{1em}{\footnotesize(row~2: $4(2,5)+(0,3)=(8,23)$)}

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{2. Back-substitution} (Lesson 2.1). The system is already upper-triangular. Solve it from the
\emph{bottom row up}:
\[
  \begin{aligned}
    x + 2y &= 5 \\
    y &= 2
  \end{aligned}
  \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad y = {\color{keyred}\mathbf{2}}, \quad x = {\color{keyred}\mathbf{1}}.
\]
\hspace{2em}{\footnotesize(bottom gives $y=2$; then $x+2(2)=5\Rightarrow x=1$)}

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{3. One multiplier} (Lesson 2.1). To clear the $x$ below the pivot in
$\ x + 3y = 7,\ \ 2x + 7y = 15$, the pivot is $1$ and the multiplier is
\[
  \ell = \frac{\text{entry to eliminate}}{\text{pivot}} = \frac{2}{1} = {\color{keyred}\mathbf{2}}.
\]
\end{notesbox}

\begin{teachernote}
Item~1 is the $LU$ shape --- lower~$\times$~upper rebuilds a full matrix, so factoring is that step run
backwards. Item~2 is the second sweep of today's method (back-substitution on $U$). Item~3's system and
multiplier ($A=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1&3\\2&7 \end{bsmallmatrix}$, $\ell=2$) are the running example
carried through the notes --- the $2$ becomes the below-diagonal entry of $L$.
\end{teachernote}
