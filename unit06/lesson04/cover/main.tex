\begin{learningtargetbox}
\small
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=5pt]
  \item \ldots{} solve a single equation $\frac{du}{dt}=\lambda u$ as $u(t)=u(0)e^{\lambda t}$, and read \textbf{growth} vs.\ \textbf{decay} from the sign of $\lambda$.
  \item \ldots{} solve a \textbf{system} $\frac{d\mathbf{u}}{dt}=A\mathbf{u}$ by splitting the start into eigenvectors: $\mathbf{u}(t)=c_1e^{\lambda_1 t}\mathbf{x}_1+c_2e^{\lambda_2 t}\mathbf{x}_2$.
  \item \ldots{} use the \textbf{signs of the eigenvalues} to decide whether a system grows, decays to a steady state, or is mixed.
\end{enumerate}
\end{learningtargetbox}

\begin{remindbox}
\small You have eigenvalues, diagonalization $A=X\Lambda X^{-1}$, and the symmetric case $S=Q\Lambda Q^{\mathsf T}$.
This lesson puts them to work on a system that \emph{changes over time}: $\frac{d\mathbf{u}}{dt}=A\mathbf{u}$.
Split the start into eigenvectors, let each piece run at its own rate $e^{\lambda t}$, and add --- the
\emph{signs} of the eigenvalues tell you whether each piece grows or decays. (Advanced/optional.)
\end{remindbox}
