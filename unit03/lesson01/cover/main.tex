\begin{remindbox}
\small A \textbf{vector space} is a set of vectors you can \textbf{add} and \textbf{scale} without
ever leaving it. A \textbf{subspace} is a subset that is itself a vector space --- so you only check
two things: it is closed under \textbf{addition} and under \textbf{scaling} (which forces
$\mathbf{0}$ inside). The \textbf{column space} $C(A)$ --- all reachable targets $A\mathbf{x}$ --- is
a subspace \emph{because} $A(\mathbf{x}+\mathbf{y})=A\mathbf{x}+A\mathbf{y}$.
\end{remindbox}
