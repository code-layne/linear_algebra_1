Solve $A\xx=\bb$ with $A=\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$ and
$\bb=\begin{bmatrix} 5 \\ 8 \end{bmatrix}$. Show your work.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=14pt]
  \item Write $A\xx=\bb$ as a system of two equations (one per row).
        \ansline{$x_1 + x_2 = 5$;\qquad $x_1 + 2x_2 = 8$}
  \item Solve the system by eliminating a variable, then \textbf{check} by rebuilding $\bb$.
        \ansline{Subtract the first from the second: $x_2 = 3$; then $x_1 = 5-3 = 2$.}
        \ansline{Check: $2\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}+3\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1\\2 \end{bsmallmatrix}=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 5\\8 \end{bsmallmatrix}$ \checkmark}
  \item Dev graphs another system and gets \textbf{two parallel lines} that never meet.
        How many solutions does that system have, and why? Connect your answer to whether the
        target $\bb$ is \emph{reachable}.
        \ansline{\textbf{No solutions.} Parallel lines never cross, so no point satisfies both
        equations. Equivalently, the columns point the same direction (a single line), and
        $\bb$ is \emph{off} that line --- unreachable --- so the system is inconsistent.}
\end{enumerate}

\begin{teachernote}
Item~3 checks the geometry--reachability link. Full credit needs both ``parallel lines don't
meet $\Rightarrow$ no solution'' \emph{and} the connection to $\bb$ not being in the column space.
\end{teachernote}
