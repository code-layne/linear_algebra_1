\begin{teachernote}[Practice Test --- Part B]
Item 2: $L$ records the elimination multipliers (with unit diagonal); $U$ holds the pivots. Item 5:
socks-and-shoes order reverses --- $(AB)^{\tp}=B^{\tp}A^{\tp}$, not $A^{\tp}B^{\tp}$.
Item 6: same line $=$ one equation is a multiple of the other (dependent) $\Rightarrow$ infinitely many;
parallel-but-different $\Rightarrow$ none.
\end{teachernote}

\begin{teachernote}[Practice Test --- Part D Scoring (5 pts each)]
D1: 1 pt (a) $ad-bc=0\Rightarrow$ singular/no inverse; 2 pts (b) infinitely many with the $0=0$
justification; 2 pts (c) none with the $0=-1$ justification. Full credit needs the \emph{reason}
(same line vs.\ parallel), not just a count. D2: 2 pts (a) correct
$A^{\tp}A=\begin{bsmallmatrix}10&5\\5&5\end{bsmallmatrix}$ and noting equal off-diagonals; 3 pts (b)
the general argument $(A^{\tp}A)^{\tp}=A^{\tp}A$ via the reversal rule. Watch for students
writing $(AB)^{\tp}=A^{\tp}B^{\tp}$ (wrong order).
\end{teachernote}

\begin{teachernote}[Unit Test --- Part B]
Item 1: $U$ carries the pivots; $L$ carries the multipliers (unit diagonal). Item 3: a permutation
undoes itself by swapping back, so $P^{\tp}=P^{-1}$. Item 6: a zero pivot that cannot be fixed by a
row swap $\Rightarrow$ singular $\Rightarrow$ no inverse ($ad-bc=0$).
\end{teachernote}

\begin{teachernote}[Unit Test --- Part D Scoring (5 pts each)]
D1: 1 pt (a) $ad-bc=0\Rightarrow$ singular/no inverse; 2 pts (b) infinitely many with the $0=0$
justification; 2 pts (c) none with the $0=-1$ justification. Full credit needs the \emph{reason}
(same line vs.\ parallel), not just a count. D2: 2 pts (a) correct
$A^{\tp}A=\begin{bsmallmatrix}5&6\\6&17\end{bsmallmatrix}$ and noting equal off-diagonals; 3 pts (b)
the general argument $(A^{\tp}A)^{\tp}=A^{\tp}A$ via the reversal rule. Watch for students
writing $(AB)^{\tp}=A^{\tp}B^{\tp}$ (wrong order).
\end{teachernote}
