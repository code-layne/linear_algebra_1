Solve $A\xx=\bb$ with $A=\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$ and
$\bb=\begin{bmatrix} 5 \\ 8 \end{bmatrix}$. Show your work.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=16pt]
  \item Write $A\xx=\bb$ as a system of two equations (one per row).
        \vspace{1.0cm}
  \item Solve the system by eliminating a variable, then \textbf{check} by rebuilding $\bb$.
        \vspace{1.4cm}
  \item Dev graphs another system and gets \textbf{two parallel lines} that never meet.
        How many solutions does that system have, and why? Connect your answer to whether the
        target $\bb$ is \emph{reachable}.
        \writelines{2}
\end{enumerate}
