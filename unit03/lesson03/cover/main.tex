\begin{remindbox}
\small The \textbf{complete solution} of $A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}$ is $\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{x}_p+\mathbf{x}_n$:
\emph{one} particular solution $\mathbf{x}_p$ plus the \emph{whole} nullspace $N(A)$. Find $\mathbf{x}_p$
by reducing the augmented matrix $[A\mid\mathbf{b}]$ and setting every free variable to $0$; attach the
special solutions from Lesson~3.2 for the nullspace part. Geometrically the solution set is the nullspace
line or plane \emph{slid over} to pass through $\mathbf{x}_p$ --- parallel to $N(A)$, through the origin
only when $\mathbf{b}=\mathbf{0}$. And $A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}$ is \textbf{solvable} exactly when
$\mathbf{b}$ lies in the column space $C(A)$.
\end{remindbox}
