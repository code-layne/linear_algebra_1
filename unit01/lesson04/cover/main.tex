\begin{remindbox}
\small To multiply \textbf{by columns}, each column of $B$ combines the columns of $A$:
$AB=\begin{bsmallmatrix} A\mathbf{b}_1 & A\mathbf{b}_2 \end{bsmallmatrix}$. To \textbf{factor}
$A=CR$, collect the \textbf{independent} columns of $A$ in $C$, then write the \textbf{recipe} $R$
that rebuilds each column of $A$ from $C$. The number of columns in $C$ is the \textbf{rank}.
\end{remindbox}
