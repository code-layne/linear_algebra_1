\begin{notesbox}{Practice}
Show your steps. For matrix products, work \emph{one column at a time}.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.9em, itemsep=10pt]
  \item Multiply by columns. Let $A=\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$.
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=4pt]
      \item $A\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = {}$\ans{$\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1 & 2\\3 & 1 \end{bsmallmatrix}$ ($AI=A$)} \hfill
      \item $A\begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = {}$\ans{$\begin{bsmallmatrix} 4 & 3\\7 & 4 \end{bsmallmatrix}$}
    \end{enumerate}
    \ (1b: col~1 $=2\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1\\3 \end{bsmallmatrix}+1\begin{bsmallmatrix} 2\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 4\\7 \end{bsmallmatrix}$; col~2 $=1\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1\\3 \end{bsmallmatrix}+1\begin{bsmallmatrix} 2\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 3\\4 \end{bsmallmatrix}$.)
  \item Factor $A=\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 5 \\ 1 & 1 & 4 \end{bmatrix}$ as $A=CR$. Which columns are
        independent? Write $C$, build the recipe $R$, and give the rank. \ansline{Columns~1 and~2 ($\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix},\begin{bsmallmatrix} 2\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}$) are independent; column~3 $=3\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}+1\begin{bsmallmatrix} 2\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 5\\4 \end{bsmallmatrix}$. $C=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1 & 2\\1 & 1 \end{bsmallmatrix}$, $R=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1 & 0 & 3\\0 & 1 & 1 \end{bsmallmatrix}$, rank $2$.}
  \item State the \textbf{rank} of each matrix and give a one-line reason.
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=4pt]
      \item $\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 4 \end{bsmallmatrix}$ \hfill
      \item $\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bsmallmatrix}$ \hfill
      \item $\begin{bsmallmatrix} 2 & 4 & 6 \\ 1 & 2 & 3 \end{bsmallmatrix}$
    \end{enumerate}
    \ansline{(a) Rank $1$ --- col~2 $=2\times$ col~1 (parallel).
    \ (b) Rank $2$ --- $\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1\\0 \end{bsmallmatrix},\begin{bsmallmatrix} 0\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}$ are independent.
    \ (c) Rank $1$ --- all three columns are multiples of $\begin{bsmallmatrix} 2\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}$.}
  \item \textbf{Reverse it.} Given $C=\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$ and
        $R=\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$, rebuild $A=CR$ by columns.
        \ans{Col~1 $=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1\\3 \end{bsmallmatrix}$, col~2 $=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 2\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}$, col~3 $=2\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1\\3 \end{bsmallmatrix}+1\begin{bsmallmatrix} 2\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 4\\7 \end{bsmallmatrix}$. So $A=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1 & 2 & 4\\3 & 1 & 7 \end{bsmallmatrix}$.}
  \item A workshop catalog is $A=\begin{bmatrix} 3 & 1 & 4 \\ 1 & 2 & 3 \end{bmatrix}$, each column a
        product listed as $\begin{bsmallmatrix} \text{parts} \\ \text{labor} \end{bsmallmatrix}$.
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=5pt]
      \item Which two products are the \emph{base} products (independent columns)? Write $C$. \ans{Products~1 and~2 --- $\begin{bsmallmatrix} 3\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}$ and $\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1\\2 \end{bsmallmatrix}$ are not multiples. $C=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 3 & 1\\1 & 2 \end{bsmallmatrix}$.}
      \item Product~3 is a bundle of the base products. Find its recipe (column~3 of $R$) and say
            what it means in parts and labor. \ansline{$\begin{bsmallmatrix} 4\\3 \end{bsmallmatrix}=1\begin{bsmallmatrix} 3\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}+1\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1\\2 \end{bsmallmatrix}$, so column~3 of $R$ is $\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}$: product~3 is one of each base product, using $4$ parts and $3$ labor.}
    \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
\end{notesbox}

\begin{extensionbox}
A factory catalog is $A=\begin{bmatrix} 2 & 4 & 6 \\ 3 & 6 & 9 \end{bmatrix}$, each column listing
$\begin{bsmallmatrix} \text{labor hours} \\ \text{material units} \end{bsmallmatrix}$. Show that the
rank is $1$, write $A=CR$ with $C$ a single column and $R$ a single row, and explain what it means
that the factory really has only \emph{one} base product.
\ansline{Every column is a multiple of $\begin{bsmallmatrix} 2\\3 \end{bsmallmatrix}$: $\begin{bsmallmatrix} 4\\6 \end{bsmallmatrix}=2\begin{bsmallmatrix} 2\\3 \end{bsmallmatrix}$, $\begin{bsmallmatrix} 6\\9 \end{bsmallmatrix}=3\begin{bsmallmatrix} 2\\3 \end{bsmallmatrix}$, so rank $1$. $C=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 2\\3 \end{bsmallmatrix}$, $R=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \end{bsmallmatrix}$. There is really one base product; every catalog item is just a scaled batch of it, always in a $2\!:\!3$ labor-to-material ratio.}
\end{extensionbox}
