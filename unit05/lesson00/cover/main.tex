\begin{remindbox}
\small A $2\times2$ matrix carries \emph{one} number, the \textbf{determinant} $ad-bc$, that reads
off two things at once: its \textbf{size} is the \emph{area} of the parallelogram the columns span,
and its \textbf{sign} tells you whether the matrix keeps or \emph{flips} orientation. When $ad-bc=0$
the columns are \emph{parallel}, the box goes flat, and the matrix has \emph{no inverse} (recall
Unit~2, where the inverse divides by $ad-bc$). That single number runs all of Unit~5.
\end{remindbox}
