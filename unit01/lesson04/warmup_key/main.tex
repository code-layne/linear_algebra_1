\begin{notesbox}{Getting Ready: Skills We'll Use Today}
\small These Lesson~1.3 moves become today's \emph{matrix product} and \emph{factorization $A=CR$}.
Answer quickly.

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{1. Combine the columns.} For $A=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1 & 3 \\ 2 & 1 \end{bsmallmatrix}$,
compute $A\begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$ as a combination of the columns.
\[
  A\begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}
  = 2\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix} + 1\begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}
  = {\color{keyred}\begin{bsmallmatrix} \mathbf{5}\\\mathbf{5} \end{bsmallmatrix}}
\]

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{2. Find the weights.} Write $\begin{bmatrix} 4 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix}$ as a combination of
$\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}$ and $\begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$:
$\begin{bmatrix} 4 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix}={\color{keyred}\mathbf{1}}\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}
+{\color{keyred}\mathbf{1}}\begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$.
\ansline{Weights $1$ and $1$: $\begin{bsmallmatrix} 1\\2 \end{bsmallmatrix}+\begin{bsmallmatrix} 3\\1 \end{bsmallmatrix}=\begin{bsmallmatrix} 4\\3 \end{bsmallmatrix}$.}

\vspace{6pt}
\textbf{3. Independent?} Are the columns $\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}$ and
$\begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$ independent (not multiples of each other)?
\ansline{Yes --- neither is a multiple of the other, so they are independent.}
