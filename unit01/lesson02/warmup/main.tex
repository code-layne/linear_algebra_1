then add the two results.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=2.0em, itemsep=8pt]

\textbf{2. Length of a vector.} Recall $\left\|\begin{bsmallmatrix} v_1 \\ v_2 \end{bsmallmatrix}\right\|
= \sqrt{v_1^2 + v_2^2}$. Find the length of $\begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix}$.
\[
  \left\|\begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix}\right\| = \blank{3.0cm}
\]
